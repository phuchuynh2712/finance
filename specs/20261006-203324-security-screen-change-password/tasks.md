### Notes

- The isolated verification client is the one new moving part: if the real-client scenario (T025) shows an unexpected effect on the app's session, stop and revisit research Decision 1 before continuing; the documented alternative is re-signing in on the main client, which was rejected for its side effects.
- `GoTrueClient.signOut(scope: others)` silently ignores 401/403/404, which is why `endOtherSessions` is guarded and the session is refreshed first (research Decision 1, step 4); do not "simplify" that away.
- If a new Dart/Flutter warning appears from `PasswordChangeGateway`'s implementation on a newer toolchain, fix it at the source rather than silencing it.
- Commit only when asked; when committing, keep US4 (configuration) separate from the product changes.
